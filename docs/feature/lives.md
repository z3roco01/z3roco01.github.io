# Lives

Lives are the most important part of this mod, without them there would be no Lifed. Players lives can either be set to a constant amount, or randomised at the start of the series, or you could do whatever with commands. Players name will be coloured with their life count, grey for <= 0, red for 1, yellow for 2, light green for 3, dark green for 4 or higher. Once a player loses their last life, they are out and put to spectator mode(but can be brought back with commands). In some series, once a player is on their last life they lose all alliances, but thats up to whoever runs the server.
